📖 ABOUT THIS SOURCE
Jefferson Davis, Farewell Address to the Senate
Author or source: Jefferson Davis
Date: January 21, 1861
Type: Speech
Citation: Delivered in the United States Senate, Washington, D.C. Printed in the Congressional Globe, 36th Congress, 2nd Session.
💡 BEFORE YOU READ

Jefferson Davis was a Mississippi planter, a West Point graduate, and a hero of the Mexican War who had served as secretary of war and as a United States senator. Twelve days after Mississippi voted to secede, he rose in the Senate to say goodbye.

Davis did not want the post of president of the new Confederacy, but within weeks he would hold it. This speech shows how one of the South's most respected leaders explained the choice, to his colleagues in the room and to the country beyond it.
