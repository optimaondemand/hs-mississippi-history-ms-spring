📖 ABOUT THIS SOURCE
The Lomax Recordings in Coahoma County
Author or source: Alan Lomax and the Fisk University researchers
Date: 1941 to 1942
Type: Sound recordings
Citation: Library of Congress, American Folklife Center: Lomax Coahoma County field recordings, 1941 to 1942.
💡 BEFORE YOU READ

In the early 1940s a team of researchers from the Library of Congress and Fisk University traveled to Coahoma County, in the Delta, to record music made by the people who lived there. Some of those they recorded, such as McKinley Morganfield, would become famous as Muddy Waters.

These recordings are sources too. They preserve voices, songs, and sounds that would otherwise be lost. Listen as a historian would, and note what you hear and what you can infer.
