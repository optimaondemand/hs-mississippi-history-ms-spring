📖 ABOUT THIS SOURCE
Mississippi's Declaration of the Causes of Secession
Author or source: The Mississippi Secession Convention
Date: January 1861
Type: Official declaration
Citation: Adopted by the state convention meeting in Jackson, Mississippi, January 1861.
💡 BEFORE YOU READ

In January 1861 delegates from every part of Mississippi met in Jackson to decide whether the state would leave the Union. The convention voted 84 to 15 to secede. The delegates then adopted a statement explaining their reasons to the public and to the other states.

A declaration like this is a rare gift to a historian. People often hide their reasons or change their story later. Here, the men who made the decision wrote down what they wanted the world to believe about it, at the moment they made it.
