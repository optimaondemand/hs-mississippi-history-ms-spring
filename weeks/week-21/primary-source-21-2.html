💡 BEFORE YOU READ

Thousands of Black Mississippians, most of them freed from slavery, joined United States Army regiments during the war. Some of these men wrote letters and petitions to officials, to newspapers, and to their families.

These documents are rare, and they let us hear in the soldiers' own words what the war meant to them.
