📖 ABOUT THIS SOURCE
The Voting Rights Act of 1965
Author or source: The United States Congress
Date: August 6, 1965
Type: Law
Citation: Voting Rights Act of 1965, Public Law 89-110.
💡 BEFORE YOU READ

After the violence of Freedom Summer and the march from Selma to Montgomery, Congress passed the Voting Rights Act. President Johnson signed it in August 1965.

The law did what earlier laws had failed to do. It put the federal government in charge of overseeing registration where states had used tests and other devices to keep people from voting. Read the sections carefully to see how.
