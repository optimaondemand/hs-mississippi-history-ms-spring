📖 ABOUT THIS SOURCE
The Mississippi Constitution of 1868: Rights and Schools
Author or source: The Mississippi Constitutional Convention of 1868
Date: 1868
Type: Constitution
Citation: Constitution of the State of Mississippi, adopted 1868. The Declaration of Rights and the Article on Education.
💡 BEFORE YOU READ

Congress's Reconstruction Acts required Mississippi to write a new constitution. The convention that met in Jackson in 1868 included Black and white delegates, and for the first time Black men took part in drafting the state's basic law.

The voters of the state turned the new constitution down the first time it was put to them, and Mississippi was not readmitted to the Union until 1870. Even so, the constitution created the state's first public school system for all children, and it stayed in force until 1890.
