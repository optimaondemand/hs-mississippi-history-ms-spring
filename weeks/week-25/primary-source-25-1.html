📖 ABOUT THIS SOURCE
The Mississippi Constitution of 1890, Article 12
Author or source: The Mississippi Constitutional Convention of 1890
Date: 1890
Type: Constitution
Citation: Constitution of the State of Mississippi, 1890, Article 12, on the right to vote.
💡 BEFORE YOU READ

In 1890 Mississippi held a constitutional convention with one great purpose: to find a way to keep Black citizens from voting. The delegates could not say so in the text, because the Fifteenth Amendment forbids denying the vote because of race.

Article 12 sets the rules for who may vote. Read it as a lawyer would, one requirement at a time, and ask who each one would stop.
