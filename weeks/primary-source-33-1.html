The Education Reform Act of 1982
Author or source: The Mississippi Legislature
Date: 1982
Type: Law
Citation: Mississippi Education Reform Act of 1982, passed in special session of the Mississippi Legislature.
💡 BEFORE YOU READ

In 1982 Mississippi had no public kindergartens and no compulsory school attendance law, and it ranked near the bottom among states on most measures of education. Governor William Winter called the Legislature into special session and pushed a package of reforms.

The Act that passed changed how Mississippi schools were run and paid for. Read it as a plan, and ask what kind of future the lawmakers imagined.
