📖 ABOUT THIS SOURCE
The Confederate Exemption Act
Author or source: The Confederate Congress
Date: October 1862
Type: Law
Citation: Act of the Congress of the Confederate States of America, October 1862, amending the Conscription Act of April 1862.
💡 BEFORE YOU READ

In 1862 the Confederacy ran short of soldiers and passed a law that drafted white men into the army. A few months later Congress passed a second law that excused certain men from service. One group it excused was overseers on large plantations.

Many Southerners called this the Twenty Negro Law, and it caused anger across the South. Read the law itself and decide whether that anger made sense.
