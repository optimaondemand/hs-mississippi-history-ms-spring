📖 ABOUT THIS SOURCE
Sherman's Report on the Meridian Expedition
Author or source: William T. Sherman
Date: 1864
Type: Official military report
Citation: Official Records of the War of the Rebellion, Series I, Volume 32.
💡 BEFORE YOU READ

In February 1864 General William T. Sherman marched a large Union force east from Vicksburg across central Mississippi to Meridian, a town where several railroads met. His goal was to wreck the railroads and anything else the Confederate army could use.

Sherman wrote a report for his superiors. It is an official document written by a commander, so it tells us what he wanted to be known, and it gives the numbers and results an army officer thought important.
