💡 BEFORE YOU READ

Mississippi's schools were desegregated through a mix of court orders, state laws, and local decisions. Some of these documents ordered schools to open to all children. Others aimed to help white families leave the public system.

The document you read here will show one part of that struggle. It is a good place to ask who wrote the rule, what they wanted, and how the rule worked in practice.
